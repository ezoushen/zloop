# Overall scope and batches

Record the initiating request, selection boundary, exclusions and completion
condition in the existing dispatch report. Distinguish a fixed issue/workstream selection
from a request to exhaust a repository, project or milestone backlog. For the latter,
retain the selection rule and refresh its membership; do not freeze the first query's
IDs or replace the goal with the easiest batch. Ordinary dispatch or `--autofix`
alone does not select a whole repository. Later user direction updates the recorded
scope and supersedes conflicting earlier alignment.
Also record whether the user requested closure of follow-ups generated by the work.
Preserve that completion intent across resumes and repairs: confirmed selected
follow-ups need verified closure or an explicit human-approved deferral. Severity,
pre-existing status, a round cap or a token budget cannot silently drop accepted
work. If a budget prevents continuation, report incomplete with the remaining work.
Batch readiness and overall completion must agree with the known outstanding items.

Each implementation/review batch remains narrow. Children of selected work split
for size inherit the parent's selection even in a fixed selection; splitting is not delivery.
Keep the parent outstanding until its required outcomes are satisfied. Complexity
or risk may justify separate batches and stronger checks, not silently dropping work.

After each batch's review and applicable verification, perform its authorized
delivery steps, then refresh the overall queue before returning. For a backlog-wide
request, triage newly opened issues and recorded follow-ups against the same selection
rule, including relevant local findings not yet published. Dedupe against canonical
issues and admit confirmed work in accepted priority order to a later batch; this
does not widen the current review. Existing broad authorization can cover that work
without another prompt. Outside the selection, retain follow-ups without acting.
Publishing issues, merging, releasing and rolling out still require the applicable
authorization; carry it forward when already granted.

Continue ready work across batches without a new dispatch invocation. Reconcile
blocked, assigned and untriaged work too; an empty ready queue is insufficient.
Close invalid, duplicate or superseded issues only with evidence and authorized
tracker transitions, never to manufacture an empty board. Finish only after a fresh,
complete query and report reconciliation show no unresolved selected work, no live
workers/reservations and all required delivery gates satisfied. If real blockers
prevent further progress, report incomplete with the remaining IDs, attempted
resolutions and exact decisions/access needed. A successful batch is not a completed
backlog. Reuse this completion condition if a native persistent goal was requested.
